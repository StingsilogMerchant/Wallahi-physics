Science corner

The ideas behind Ana’s business

Main idea: the circular economy

Instead of make, use, and throw away, a circular economy keeps materials in use. Ana’s bags follow this loop:

  1. Collect scrap fabric from tailors and neighbors.
  2. Design bags that use every piece.
  3. Sell to locals and tourists.
  4. Take back old bags and turn them into new ones.

Tensile strength

This is how much pulling a material can take before it snaps. Ana picks strong woven straps so a full bag doesn’t rip. Fun fact: braided fibers are often stronger than single threads.

Friction and aerodynamics

Car makers shape bodies to slip through air with less drag, and tires use friction to grip the road. Less drag means less fuel burned, which is good for business and for the planet.

Supply and demand
